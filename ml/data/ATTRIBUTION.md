# Training data attribution

`iubat_training.csv` was derived with `python -m ml.prepare_iubat` from:

> "Dataset on Academic Performance of Computer Science Students at Selected Universities in Bangladesh", Mendeley Data (https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/ns87rtkv58/2), licence CC BY 4.0.

It was changed (columns renamed, values cleaned and converted) as described at the top of `ml/prepare_iubat.py`.
It contains no names or matric numbers. It is NOT data from your own university.
