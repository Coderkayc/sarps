Rules and assumptions

SARPS estimates, from a student's own answers, whether they are at risk academically. This page lists every assumption behind that estimate.

1. The prediction model (machine learning)

2. Assumed, not confirmed

  • Every answer is the student's own report. SARPS does not receive official attendance, CA or exam records, so none of them is verified.
  • "About 75% attendance to sit exams" is mentioned in the advice as a commonly required level. It has not been confirmed for UNN.
  • CA is taken as 30 of the 100 marks in each course.
  • Advice sentences come from fixed rules that quote the student's own answers. Nothing in them is generated freely.
