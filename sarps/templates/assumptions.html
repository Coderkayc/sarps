Rules and assumptions

SARPS mixes two different things. This page says which is which and lists every assumption.

1. Exact UNN arithmetic (not a prediction)

Grades, GPA, CGPA, degree class and "what do I need in the exam" are calculated by fixed rules. You can check them by hand.

Continuous assessment (CA) is out of 30 and the exam out of 70. Degree classes: {% for low, name in classes %}{{ name }} from {{ "%.2f"|format(low) }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}.

Source: the UNN Faculty of Arts page arts.unn.edu.ng/?p=271 (grade bands, CA/exam split, degree classes).

Assumed, not confirmed

  • Grade points A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1, F=0. The page above only shows A, C, D and E.
  • Every course is marked CA out of 30 and exam out of 70.
  • GPA and CGPA are weighted by credit units and rounded half up to two decimals; half marks round up.
  • Computer Science uses the same scale as the Faculty of Arts page.
  • The current semester is set by the administrator, not detected automatically.
  • "About 75% attendance to sit exams" is mentioned in advice as a commonly required level. It has not been confirmed for UNN.

3. Whose figures count

Course figures on the Courses page are used for the plan arithmetic only. A figure you type yourself is labelled unverified. The system is built so that an official figure (from a lecturer, the ICT office or a class rep) would always replace yours and differences would be flagged, but staff entry of official figures is not built yet, so at the moment every figure there is your own.
