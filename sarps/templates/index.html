How results are classified

Probability meter Shown after you submit an assessment
  • At Risk probability of Not at Risk is 50% or below
  • Not at Risk probability of Not at Risk is above 50%

How it works

Three simple steps, repeatable any time during the semester.

1

Enter your details

Fill in a short form about your attendance, assignments and study habits.

2

Get an assessment

A machine-learning model estimates the probability that you are Not at Risk and classifies you as At Risk or Not at Risk.

3

Track your history

Submit new assessments as the semester moves on and compare how things change.

What you will enter

Some fields are required, others are optional.

Required Mandatory

  • Class attendance
  • Assignment completion

Optional Optional

  • Previous CGPA (you can mark it as unavailable)
  • Financial capability
  • Average study hours per week

Who uses SARPS

Each role sees only what it is allowed to see.

Student

Views only their own assessments and history.

Academic adviser

Views assessments of the students assigned to them.

Lecturer

Views assessments of the students assigned to them.

Head of Department

Views assessments of students in their department.

Administrator

Manages accounts and assignments. Cannot view assessments.

Sharing and privacy

Before every assessment you must agree to how it is shared. If an assessment is classified as At Risk, it is flagged for the academic staff authorised to see it, so that support can be offered.
