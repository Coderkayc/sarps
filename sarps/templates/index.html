About

A gentle early warning for your semester.

Check in as often as you like. Each assessment is saved, so you can see how things change.

📈

Quick risk assessment

Answer a short form about attendance, assignments, tests, study habits and finances, and see your result in seconds.

🕓

Track your history

Submit a new assessment whenever your situation changes and compare it with the earlier ones.

🤝

Support when you need it

If an assessment is classified At Risk, it is flagged for the academic staff authorised to see it, so help can reach you early.

How it works

Three simple steps

Repeatable any time during the semester.

1

Enter your details

Answer a short form about attendance, assignments, tests, study habits and finances.

2

Get an assessment

A machine-learning model estimates the probability that you are Not at Risk and labels you AT RISK or NOT AT RISK.

3

Track your history

Submit new assessments as the semester moves on and see how things change.

What you will answer

Five short sections, plus your CGPA if you have one

Class attendance

Pick the option that best describes how often you attend.

Assignment submission

How many were assigned, and how many you have submitted.

Tests and CAs

How many you have done and how you felt you did. Your honest impression is enough.

Study hours per week

Pick your typical weekly study time outside class.

Financial situation

How comfortable you are, and how often money gets in the way.

CGPA

Enter it if you have one. If you do not, just say so: it is never counted as zero.

Who uses {{ app_name }}

Each role sees only what it is allowed to see

Student

Views only their own assessments and history.

Academic adviser

Views assessments of the students assigned to them.

Lecturer

Views assessments of the students assigned to them.

Head of Department

Views assessments of students in their department.

Administrator

Manages accounts and assignments. Cannot view assessments.

Privacy

Sharing and privacy

Before every assessment you must agree to how it is shared. If an assessment is classified as At Risk, it is flagged for the academic staff authorised to see it, so that support can be offered.
